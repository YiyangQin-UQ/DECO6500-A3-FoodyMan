# FoodyMan A3 evidence archive

DECO6500 Assessment 3, The University of Queensland. Author: Yiyang Qin. Intended report reader: the DECO6500 teaching team.

This method case study examines how consideration for others and the effort of coordination affect decisions about jointly purchased food. It focuses on Layer 3.1 and uses three scenario interviews conducted on 28 October 2026, with COM-B supporting analysis.

## Publication and source status

The researcher confirmed on 7 November 2026 that all three participants separately and explicitly authorised public release of their de-identified full interview transcripts. The transcripts preserve original wording and timestamp order, with any publication redaction marked. Automatic transcription and checks are described in each file. The record has not received full human audio verification.

This repository serves this assessment and its evidence checking. Interview material is not offered under an unrestricted reuse licence. Audio recordings, signatures, contact details and the participant identity key remain local. P01's group-chat dataset is not included; the supplementary account is the researcher's participation reflection. Third-party course documents and articles are linked at their official sources.

A2 extracts are selected summaries with PDF page references, not a copy of the complete notebook. The original A2 report remains in the course submission record. A2 and A3 participant identifiers refer to separate studies.
